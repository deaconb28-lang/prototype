# Reference library

The design knowledge the styles and skills are built on. It holds **links and written observations only**. The repo is public, so no Mobbin images are stored here; open a reference's link on Mobbin to see it (a Mobbin account is required).

- **177 section references** in `sections/` (15 section types), collected 2026-11-11 through the Mobbin MCP. Each has the layout, type, color (approximate values), crafted details, why it works and how a trade business would adapt it, plus per-section `takeaways` and `avoid` lists.
- **13 style directions** in `styles-research/directions.json`, whole-page visual systems with cited references. Ten of them became `styles/`.
- **35 real trade-business sites and 38 conversion patterns** in `trade-patterns.json` (web research), plus quote-form fields and trust signals per trade, copy patterns, anti-patterns and schema.org types.

The format is in [SCHEMA.md](SCHEMA.md). Refresh or extend it with `/refresh-library`.

## Style directions

- **Editorial Warmth** (`editorial-warm`): A small-press magazine feel: light condensed serif with one italic word, warm cream paper, terracotta accents and photos that sit inset like printed plates.
- **Cinematic Horizon** (`cinematic-horizon`): Big-sky, full-viewport landscape or aerial photography with a calm white serif headline centered in the light, like an opening film still.
- **Jobsite Industrial** (`jobsite-industrial`): Tall condensed caps, concrete and black, hi-vis yellow and safety orange: built like signage on a work truck or a tool catalog.
- **Poster Pop** (`poster-pop`): Loud, warm and friendly: giant colored condensed headline on cream, sticker shapes, cobalt pills, like a screen-printed neighborhood poster.
- **Swiss Grid** (`swiss-grid`): White page, black neo-grotesk set huge and tight on a visible grid, with a red registration mark as the only color: precise and quietly expensive.
- **Blueprint Spec** (`blueprint-spec`): A technical spec sheet: monospace caps, bracketed buttons, guide lines, footnotes and measurements, for trades that sell precision.
- **Nocturne Premium** (`nocturne-premium`): Near-black, refined serif with an italic turn, hairline geometry and warm low light: for high-ticket jobs that want to feel like a private studio.
- **Field Guide** (`field-guide`): Deep olive and moss fields, soft serif headlines and big landscape imagery, with an orange trail-marker accent: the outdoors as a printed guidebook.
- **Market Fresh** (`market-fresh`): Fresh and dependable: cream page, forest-green ink, sage tint bands, hairline borders and a lime pill, like a good neighborhood grocer.
- **Soft Rounded** (`soft-rounded`): Gentle and reassuring: blush, peach and butter color fields, big rounded cards, a soft serif headline and dark-cocoa ink.
- **Heritage Ledger** (`heritage-ledger`): Established and trustworthy: classic book serif in oxblood-brown on paper, ledger rules, mono figures, a signature script and torn-document details.
- **Wide Stance** (`wide-stance`): Modern contractor confidence: extra-wide heavy display in navy and slate, rounded photo plates, solid navy blocks and hard numbers.
- **Workshop Craft** (`workshop-craft`): Rugged and hand-made: chunky slab serif on grainy kraft paper, torn edges, stitched borders and real crew photos with name tags.

## Top conversion patterns on real trade sites

- **Every phone number is a tap-to-call link** (34/35 have tel: links somewhere (BOXT is the exception). Colorlib: tappable anywhere on 17/18 plumbing, 27/30 HVAC, 19/27 roofing and only 13/19 electrician sites.): Every printed number is an <a href="tel:+1XXXXXXXXXX"> link (E.164 in the href, local format in the text).
- **Printed phone number in the header, with when it's answered** (31/35 print a tappable number in the header. Misses: Bumble, BOXT, Aspect (icon only), Neil Kelly. Colorlib: phone above the fold on 16/18 plumbing, 25/30 HVAC, 17/27 roofing, 10/19 electrician sites.): Top-right of the header on desktop, top bar on mobile.
- **Sticky call/quote bar on mobile** (At least 7/35 visible in the page source (Bone Dry, Fallon, Nozzle Nolen, A1 Garage, Powerful Electrical, Garage Door Fix, Normandy's sticky header). This is a lower bound because pages were read as text. Also cited: A-1 Total Service Plumbing's 'fixed Call Now bar' (Colorlib), Retro Roofers' sticky header and MHI's floating review button (ServiceTitan), Jansen Roofing's sticky free-estimate tab (Hook Agency).): On mobile, a bar fixed to the bottom of the screen with 2 (at most 3) equal-width buttons: [Call] [Free Quote/Book], and optionally [Text].
- **Two paths in the hero: quote/book plus call** (About 11/35 have both actions in the hero itself (Hedlund, Snell, Arrow, DYAD, Powerful, Service Today, Fallon, A1 Garage, Garage Door Fix, Anderson, Pimlico). Unbounce: home-improvement pages offering mixed CTAs (click, form, phone) convert at 4% vs a 2.6% baseline.): The hero offers one main action (quote, book or estimate) and one secondary action that shows the phone number.
- **Emergency / 24-7 availability banner** (14/35 overall. Plumbing/HVAC/multi-trade 9/10, roofing 2/7, electrical 1/3, garage doors 1/2. Colorlib: 16/18 plumbing, 24/30 HVAC, but only 5/19 electrician sites.): Emergency trades get a top strip or header label that says when calls are answered and by whom: 'Call 843-300-9675, answered 24/7 by a real person' (Powerful); '24/7 availability on nights, weekends, and holidays at no extra cost' (Snell); 'for no heat furnace repair situations' (Ignite, seasonal).
- **A response or arrival time the customer can check** (11/35 make some same-day or response promise; 7/35 give a number or a cut-off time.): Put a concrete, checkable time promise next to the CTA.
- **Name the free thing in the CTA** (21/35 overall; roofing 6/7 (Colorlib: free inspection on 19/27 roofing sites). Sites without it swap in published or instant pricing (BOXT, Tidy Casa, Maid Marines, Garage Door Fix, A1 estimator) or a disclosed call-out fee (Fallon).): Put the free offer in the button text: 'Get My Free Estimate', 'Schedule Free Inspection', 'FREE 2nd Opinion on Major HVAC Repairs', 'FREE Electrical Panel Inspection', and add 'no obligation'.
- **Financing shown as a monthly payment** (17/35 mention financing: roofing 6/7, HVAC/heating 7/7, remodeling 2/3. Only 4/35 show an actual monthly figure. Colorlib: roofing 12/27; HVAC 'pricing or financing' 23/30.): Next to big-ticket services, show a monthly figure, the lender's name and a line saying checking won't affect credit: 'New HVAC System for as little as $113/month' (Snell); 'HVAC Systems Replacements Starting at $99/mo.' (Hiller); Tadlock's '$132/mo' example; 'with no impact to your credit score for checking options' (Guardian/Upgrade); '0% Interest payment plans available through Brighte' (Fallon).
- **Named service area and a page per town** (31/35 name their area (misses: BOXT, Bright, DYAD, Gibbs); about 20/35 link to town/county pages (Houston Roofing 33, Superior 29, Pimlico 32 boroughs). Colorlib: area named on 13/18 plumbing, 24/30 HVAC, 15/27 roofing, 10/19 electrician sites.): Name real towns on the homepage instead of 'and surrounding areas', grouped by sub-region when there are many (Hedlund: 'Seattle & North End', 'The Eastside').
- **Reviews with rating, count and source** (12/35 show a rating or count with a named source; 21/35 show some aggregate; 14/35 show none (Snell, Hiller, Tadlock, Service Today, among others). BrightLocal 2026: 97% read reviews, 68% need 4+ stars, 47% won't use a business with under 20 reviews, 74% want reviews from the last 3 months.): Put an aggregate badge in the header or hero: stars + count + platform + a link to the profile ('4.9' / '335+ Google reviews' linked to Google Maps; 'Google rating 5.0 from 2,352 reviews').
- **Printed licence and insurance numbers** (12/35 print licence numbers; about 25/35 claim licensed/insured/accredited in some form. Colorlib: real numbers on only 5/30 HVAC and 5/19 electrician sites.): Print the licence number itself, not just 'Licensed & Insured': 'Lic.
- **Manufacturer and dealer certifications** (13/35 overall; roofing 7/7.): A row of manufacturer badges with the tier name and what it means for the customer: GAF Master Elite (GAF says fewer than 2% of roofers qualify; unlocks Golden Pledge warranty), Owens Corning Platinum Preferred, CertainTeed ShingleMaster; HVAC dealer status (Trane, Napoleon 'authorized dealer', Carrier, Lennox); Colorbond 'authorised installer'; hardscape (Keystone authorised); garage doors (LiftMaster, 'authorized Sommer dealer'); EV chargers (Tesla, Wallbox, Zappi)..
