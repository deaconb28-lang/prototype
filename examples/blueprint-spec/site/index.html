Questions

Common questions

Don't see yours? Call (614) 555-0117 and ask.

How much does a new AC or furnace cost?

It depends on the size of the house, the efficiency you choose and whether the ductwork needs changes. Central AC replacements commonly run $5,000 to $12,000 installed, and gas furnaces $3,000 to $7,500. Heat pumps vary more. We quote in writing after measuring your house, with a few options to compare.

Should I repair or replace my system?

A common rule of thumb: if the system is over 12 to 15 years old and the repair costs more than a third of a new one, replacement usually makes more sense. Frequent breakdowns and rising bills point the same way. We'll price both so you can decide.

My AC uses R-22. Can it still be fixed?

R-22 hasn't been produced or imported in the US since 2020, so what's left is expensive. Electrical and fan repairs are still worth doing. A refrigerant leak on an R-22 system is usually the point where replacement makes more sense.

What should I check before I call?

Make sure the thermostat is set to heat or cool and has fresh batteries, the filter isn't clogged and the breaker for the system hasn't tripped. For a furnace, check that the power switch near it is on. If none of that fixes it, call us.

How often should I change my filter?

Check it monthly. Basic 1-inch filters usually need changing every one to three months, more often with pets or heavy use. Thicker 4 to 5 inch media filters typically last six months to a year.
