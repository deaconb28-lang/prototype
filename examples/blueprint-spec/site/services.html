Services

Heating and Air Conditioning services in Columbus

Everything we do, with what's included. Call (614) 555-0117 if you don't see your job here.

  • 01

    AC repair

    We check the whole system, from the thermostat to the outdoor unit, before we name a cause. Common fixes like capacitors, contactors, fan motors and clogged drain lines are usually done the same visit from parts on the truck. If it's low on refrigerant, we find the leak before we add more.

    • Capacitor, contactor and fan motor replacement
    • Refrigerant leak detection and repair
    • Frozen coil and clogged condensate line fixes
    • All major brands
    More on ac repair

  • 02

    Furnace and heating repair

    Most no-heat calls come down to an igniter, flame sensor, pressure switch or blower motor. We test the system, check for carbon monoxide and give you a flat price before replacing anything.

    • Gas and electric furnaces, boilers and heat pumps
    • Igniter, flame sensor and blower repairs
    • Carbon monoxide and heat exchanger checks
    • Thermostat diagnosis and replacement
    More on furnace and heating repair

  • 03

    Tune-ups and maintenance

    A tune-up is a cleaning and a checkup. We clean the coils and burners, test the electrical parts and safety controls, check refrigerant pressures and clear the drain line. You get a written list of anything wearing out, with no pressure to fix it that day.

    • Coil, burner and drain line cleaning
    • Electrical and safety control testing
    • Refrigerant and airflow checks
    • Filter change and written condition report
    More on tune-ups and maintenance

  • 04

    AC replacement

    We size the new system with a Manual J load calculation instead of copying the old unit's size. Most replacements take one day, including the line set, pad, electrical disconnect and startup testing. We handle the permit and register the warranty.

    • Manual J load calculation before we quote
    • Two or three options with efficiency ratings explained
    • Permit, startup testing and haul-away included
    • Manufacturer warranty registered for you
    More on ac replacement

  • 05

    Furnace replacement

    If your furnace is 15 to 20 years old or needs a repair that costs more than it's worth, we'll price a replacement next to the repair so you can compare. We handle the venting, gas connection, permit and inspection.

    • 80% and 95%+ efficiency gas furnaces
    • Venting and condensate changes for high-efficiency units
    • Carbon monoxide test at startup
    • Old unit removed and recycled
    More on furnace replacement

  • 06

    Heat pumps

    A heat pump moves heat instead of burning fuel, so one system handles both heating and cooling. In colder areas we pair it with a gas furnace as backup or install a cold-climate model. We'll show you how operating costs compare with what you have now.

    • Ducted and ductless heat pumps
    • Dual-fuel setups with a gas furnace backup
    • Cold-climate models rated for low temperatures
    • Utility rebate paperwork where available
    More on heat pumps

  • 07

    Ductless mini-splits

    Mini-splits mount on the wall or ceiling and connect to a small outdoor unit through a hole about three inches wide. Each room gets its own control, and there's no ductwork to build. Most single-room installs take a day.

    • Single and multi-zone systems
    • Wall, ceiling cassette and slim ducted indoor units
    • Line set covers for a clean exterior
    • A good fit for additions, sunrooms and finished garages
    More on ductless mini-splits

  • 08

    Indoor air quality and ductwork

    If some rooms are always too hot or too cold, or the house is dusty no matter how often you clean, the ductwork is often the cause. We test airflow, seal and repair leaky ducts and add filtration or humidity control where it helps.

    • Media filters and air cleaners
    • Whole-home humidifiers and dehumidifiers
    • Duct sealing, repair and new runs
    • Room-by-room airflow testing
    More on indoor air quality and ductwork
