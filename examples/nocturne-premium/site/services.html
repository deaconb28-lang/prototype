Services

Remodeling services in Scottsdale

Everything we do, with what's included. Call (480) 555-0192 if you don't see your job here.

  • 01

    Bathroom remodeling

    We take the bathroom down to the studs when needed, fix what's behind the walls and rebuild with a proper waterproofing system behind the tile. Most full bathroom remodels take three to six weeks once materials are on site.

    • Tub-to-shower conversions
    • Waterproofing membrane behind all shower tile
    • Vanities, tile, lighting and exhaust fans
    • Curbless showers and grab bars
    More on bathroom remodeling

  • 02

    Kitchen remodeling

    We start with how you cook and use the room, then plan the layout, cabinets, counters, lighting and appliances. Most kitchen remodels take six to twelve weeks of construction, and we can set up a temporary kitchen so you can still make coffee.

    • Design and layout planning
    • Cabinets, countertops and backsplash
    • Electrical, plumbing and lighting upgrades
    • Temporary kitchen setup during the work
    More on kitchen remodeling

  • 03

    Basement finishing

    We check for moisture before we frame anything, because finishing over a damp wall leads to mold. Then we frame, insulate, wire, drywall and finish, with egress windows where a bedroom needs one.

    • Moisture check and fixes before framing
    • Egress windows for bedrooms
    • Bathrooms, wet bars and media rooms
    • Permits and inspections at each stage
    More on basement finishing

  • 04

    Accessibility and aging-in-place

    Small changes can let you stay in your home longer: a curbless shower, grab bars anchored to blocking in the wall, wider doorways, better lighting and lever handles. We plan around how you and your family actually move through the house.

    • Curbless and walk-in showers
    • Grab bars with blocking in the wall
    • Wider doorways and ramps
    • First-floor bedroom and bath conversions
    More on accessibility and aging-in-place

  • 05

    Flooring and interior updates

    Not every project is a full remodel. We replace flooring, install new trim and doors, build shelves and built-ins and work through the list of smaller jobs that have been waiting.

    • Hardwood, luxury vinyl plank and tile flooring
    • Trim, baseboards and interior doors
    • Built-in shelves and window seats
    • Small projects grouped into one visit
    More on flooring and interior updates

  • 06

    Home additions

    We plan additions with an architect or designer so the roofline, siding and trim match the house. We handle engineering, permits, foundation, framing and finishes, and give you a schedule with milestones. Most additions take three to six months from groundbreaking.

    • Family rooms, bedrooms and primary suites
    • Bump-outs to enlarge a kitchen or bath
    • Second-story additions
    • Engineering, permits and inspections handled
    More on home additions

  • 07

    Whole-home renovations

    In a whole-home renovation, the order of work matters: structure, then mechanicals, then insulation, then finishes. We plan the full scope up front, give you a schedule by phase and tell you early if something behind the walls changes the plan.

    • Full design and scope before demo
    • Electrical, plumbing and HVAC updates
    • Insulation and window upgrades
    • Change orders in writing before work continues
    More on whole-home renovations
