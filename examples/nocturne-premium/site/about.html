About Ashgrove Build Co.

Ashgrove Build Co. We handle design, permits and every trade, put the price and schedule in writing and keep one project lead on your job from start to finish.

What we stand behind

Our guarantees

  • Workmanship warranty: 2 years, in writing

  • Fixed price for the agreed scope; any change priced in writing before work

  • Weekly progress updates

  • Punch list finished before the final payment

Licenses and certifications

  • NARI Certified Remodeler (CR)
  • NKBA Certified Kitchen Designer (CKD)
  • NAHB Certified Aging-in-Place Specialist (CAPS)
  • EPA Lead-Safe Certified Firm

How it works

What happens after you call

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We meet at your home, talk through what you want and give you a realistic budget range.

  2. 02

    Design and fixed price

    We finalize the plans and selections and give you a written contract with the price and schedule.

  3. 03

    Build

    Your project lead runs the trades and inspections and sends you an update every week.

  4. 04

    Final walk-through

    We walk the finished space with you, make a punch list and finish every item on it.
