Basement finishing in Scottsdale

Unfinished basements turned into living space, with moisture handled first.

What to expect

We check for moisture before we frame anything, because finishing over a damp wall leads to mold. Then we frame, insulate, wire, drywall and finish, with egress windows where a bedroom needs one.

What's included

  • Moisture check and fixes before framing
  • Egress windows for bedrooms
  • Bathrooms, wet bars and media rooms
  • Permits and inspections at each stage

Common questions

Don't see yours? Call (480) 555-0192 and ask.

How much does a kitchen or bathroom remodel cost?

It depends on size, layout changes and the finishes you choose. National cost surveys put a mid-range bathroom remodel around $25,000, a minor kitchen update around $28,000 and a full mid-range kitchen remodel closer to $80,000. We give you a budget range at the first meeting and a fixed price once the design is set.

How long will my remodel take?

Most full bathrooms take three to six weeks of construction, kitchens six to twelve weeks and additions several months. Design, permits and ordering materials add time up front. We give you a written schedule before we start and update it weekly.

Do you pull permits?

Yes. Work involving structure, electrical, plumbing or HVAC needs permits in most places. We pull them, schedule the inspections and include the cost in your price. Unpermitted work can cause problems with insurance and when you sell.

Can we live at home during the remodel?

Usually, yes. We seal off the work area with dust barriers, keep a clean path through the house and set up a temporary kitchen for kitchen remodels. For whole-home renovations, some families move out for part of the job, and we'll tell you if we think that's worth it.
