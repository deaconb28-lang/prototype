Questions

Common questions

Are the treatments safe for my kids and pets?

We use EPA-registered products and apply them according to the label, which is the law. For most exterior treatments, keep kids and pets off treated areas until they dry, usually an hour or two. Before we leave, we tell you what we used and any steps to take.

How much does pest control cost?

The first visit usually costs more than follow-ups because it includes a full inspection and a heavier treatment. Ongoing quarterly service for an average home commonly runs $100 to $300 per visit. Termites, bed bugs and rodent exclusion are priced by the job after we inspect.

Why quarterly service instead of one treatment?

Exterior treatments break down over a few months from sun and rain, and new pests keep arriving from outside. Quarterly visits keep the barrier in place. For a one-time problem, like a single wasp nest, a one-time visit is fine.

Do I need to leave the house during treatment?

For most general pest treatments, no. For bed bug heat treatments and some flea or roach treatments, you'll need to be out for a few hours, and we'll tell you ahead of time.

How do I know if I have termites?

Look for mud tubes on the foundation, piles of discarded wings near windows in spring, wood that sounds hollow or paint that bubbles. Many homes show no signs until the damage turns up during a repair. An annual inspection is the most reliable way to catch them.
