Services

Pest Control services in Tampa

Everything we do, with what's included. Call (813) 555-0134 if you don't see your job here.

  • 01

    General pest control

    We inspect the house for entry points and nesting areas, treat the foundation, eaves and entry points outside, and treat inside only where we find activity. Most customers go on a quarterly plan so the barrier stays in place all year.

    • Exterior perimeter treatment
    • Interior spot treatments where needed
    • Web and nest removal from eaves
    • Free return visits between scheduled treatments
    More on general pest control

  • 02

    Rodent control

    Traps take care of the mice and rats inside, but more will follow unless the gaps are closed. A mouse can squeeze through an opening about a quarter inch wide, so we check around pipes, vents, garage doors and the foundation, seal openings with metal mesh and sealant, and come back to check the traps.

    • Inspection for entry points and droppings
    • Snap traps and tamper-resistant bait stations
    • Exclusion work with metal mesh and sealant
    • Follow-up visits to check and clear traps
    More on rodent control

  • 03

    Wasps, hornets and bees

    Yellow jackets and hornets defend their nests hard, especially in late summer. We treat and remove nests safely, including ones inside walls and in the ground. Honey bee swarms go to a local beekeeper whenever they can be relocated.

    • Paper wasp and hornet nest removal
    • Ground and wall yellow jacket nests
    • Honey bee referrals to local beekeepers
    • Same-day visits when a nest is near a door
    More on wasps, hornets and bees

  • 04

    Termite treatment

    Subterranean termites can do damage for years before anyone sees it. We inspect the foundation, crawlspace and wood near the soil for mud tubes and damage, then recommend a liquid treatment, a bait system or both, backed by a warranty you can renew each year.

    • Full-home termite inspection
    • Liquid soil treatment around the foundation
    • Bait station installation and monitoring
    • Annual renewable warranty
    More on termite treatment

  • 05

    Mosquito and tick control

    We treat the shady, damp places where mosquitoes rest (shrubs, under decks, along fences) and dump or treat standing water where they breed. Most programs run every 3 to 4 weeks during mosquito season.

    • Barrier treatments on foliage and resting areas
    • Standing water checks and larvicide
    • Tick treatment along wood lines and tall grass
    • One-time treatments before outdoor events
    More on mosquito and tick control

  • 06

    Bed bug treatment

    Bed bugs hide in mattress seams, furniture joints and wall gaps, so treatment has to be thorough. We confirm it's bed bugs, treat with heat, products or both, and come back to check. You get a prep checklist before the visit, because how the room is prepared matters.

    • Inspection and identification
    • Whole-room heat treatment or targeted products
    • Prep checklist before treatment
    • Follow-up inspection included
    More on bed bug treatment

  • 07

    Termite inspections for home sales

    Many lenders and buyers need a wood-destroying insect (WDI) report before closing. We inspect the home, fill out the standard form used in your state and send it to you, your agent and the lender, usually within a day of the inspection.

    • NPMA-33 or your state's required WDI form
    • Report sent to agents and lenders
    • Scheduling built around closing deadlines
    • Treatment quote if we find activity
    More on termite inspections for home sales
