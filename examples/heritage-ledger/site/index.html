Questions

Common questions

Don't see yours? Call (804) 555-0163 and ask.

How much does a plumber cost?

It depends on the job. Clearing a single drain usually runs $150 to $450, and replacing a standard tank water heater usually runs $1,500 to $3,500 installed. We give you a flat price before we start, so you aren't watching the clock.

Do you charge a trip or diagnostic fee?

We tell you the service call fee on the phone before we come out, so there are no surprises. Ask us whether it goes toward the repair if you go ahead with the work.

What should I do while I wait for you?

Turn off the water at the main shutoff (often in the basement, garage or near the water meter) or at the small valve under the fixture. If water is near outlets or appliances, shut off power to that area. Move what you can out of the way and take photos for insurance.

I smell gas. What should I do?

Leave the house first. Don't flip switches or use your phone inside. Call your gas utility or 911 from outside, and once they've made it safe, we can find and fix the leak.

Should I repair or replace my water heater?

If the tank itself is leaking, it needs replacing. If it's a thermostat, element, gas valve or igniter and the heater is under about 8 years old, a repair usually makes sense. Past 10 to 12 years, replacement is often the better spend.
