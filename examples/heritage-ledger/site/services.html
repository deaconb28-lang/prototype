Services

Plumbing services in Richmond

Everything we do, with what's included. Call (804) 555-0163 if you don't see your job here.

  • 01

    Drain cleaning

    We clear the clog with a cable machine or a hydro-jetter depending on what's in the line, then run water to make sure it drains the way it should. If the same drain keeps backing up, we can run a camera to see whether it's roots, grease or a broken pipe.

    • Kitchen, bath, laundry and floor drains
    • Main line clearing from the cleanout
    • Hydro-jetting for grease and heavy buildup
    • Camera inspection when clogs keep coming back
    More on drain cleaning

  • 02

    Leak and pipe repair

    A small leak inside a wall can do more damage than a burst pipe you can see. We find the source, repair or replace the damaged section and tell you if the rest of that pipe is likely to fail the same way.

    • Copper, PEX, CPVC and galvanized pipe repair
    • Shutoff valve replacement
    • Frozen and burst pipe repair
    • Leak detection in walls, ceilings and slabs
    More on leak and pipe repair

  • 03

    Water heaters

    Most tank water heaters last 8 to 12 years. If yours is leaking or running out of hot water, we'll tell you whether a repair makes sense or a replacement is the better spend. Most tank swaps are done the same day, and we haul the old one away.

    • Gas and electric tank heaters, 40 to 80 gallons
    • Tankless installs, including gas line and venting changes
    • Expansion tanks and other code-required parts
    • Permit and haul-away included in the price
    More on water heaters

  • 04

    Toilets, faucets and disposals

    These are small jobs, but they waste water and money when they're left alone. We repair what can be repaired and install new fixtures, whether you bought them or want us to supply them.

    • Toilet repair and replacement
    • Kitchen and bath faucet installs
    • Garbage disposal repair and replacement
    • Shower valve and cartridge replacement
    More on toilets, faucets and disposals

  • 05

    Sewer line repair

    Gurgling drains, sewage smells or backups in the lowest drain in the house usually point to the main sewer line. We run a camera down the line, show you the video and give you options from a spot repair to full replacement, including trenchless methods where the line allows.

    • Video camera inspection with a recording you keep
    • Root cutting and hydro-jetting
    • Spot repairs and full line replacement
    • Trenchless lining or pipe bursting where it fits
    More on sewer line repair

  • 06

    Sump pumps

    A sump pump only matters if it works during the worst storm of the year. We install and replace pumps, check the float and check valve, and add a battery backup so it keeps running when the power goes out.

    • Primary pump replacement
    • Battery and water-powered backup pumps
    • Check valves and discharge lines
    • High-water alarms
    More on sump pumps

  • 07

    Gas lines

    Gas work needs a licensed plumber and, in most places, a permit and an inspection. We run new lines, add shutoff valves and pressure-test every connection before the gas goes back on.

    • New lines for ranges, dryers, fireplaces and grills
    • Generator and pool heater hookups
    • Pressure testing and leak checks
    • Permit and inspection scheduled for you
    More on gas lines

  • 08

    Whole-house repiping

    Low water pressure, rusty water or leaks popping up in different places can mean the pipes themselves are worn out. We repipe with PEX or copper, open as little drywall as we can and can patch the openings when we're done.

    • PEX or copper supply lines
    • Most homes done in two to five days
    • Water back on at the end of each day where possible
    • Drywall patching available
    More on whole-house repiping
