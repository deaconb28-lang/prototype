Questions

Common questions

How much does house cleaning cost?

It depends on the size of the home, how often we come and its condition. Recurring cleaning for an average home usually runs $120 to $250 per visit. A first deep clean or a move-out clean costs more, often $250 to $500 or more. We give you a flat price before the first visit.

What isn't included in a regular cleaning?

Regular visits don't include the inside of the oven or fridge, interior windows, laundry or moving heavy furniture, though you can add most of those. We don't clean up biohazards, pest droppings or mold, and we don't work above step-stool height.
