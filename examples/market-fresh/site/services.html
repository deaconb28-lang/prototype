Services

House Cleaning services in Raleigh

Everything we do, with what's included. Call (919) 555-0176 if you don't see your job here.

  • 01

    Recurring house cleaning

    Every visit covers the kitchen, bathrooms, floors, dusting and the rooms you live in most, following the same checklist so nothing gets skipped. You can leave notes for the team and rotate in extras like baseboards or the inside of the microwave.

    • Kitchen: counters, sink, appliance fronts and stovetop
    • Bathrooms: toilets, tubs, showers and mirrors
    • Dusting, vacuuming and mopping throughout
    • Beds made and trash taken out
    More on recurring house cleaning

  • 02

    Deep cleaning

    A deep clean takes longer than a regular visit because it covers the details: baseboards, door frames, light switches, cabinet fronts, vents and built-up soap scum and grease. Most new recurring customers start with one, so regular visits can keep the house at that level.

    • Baseboards, door frames and trim
    • Cabinet fronts and backsplash
    • Soap scum, hard water spots and grease
    • Vents, light fixtures and ceiling fans
    More on deep cleaning

  • 03

    Move-in and move-out cleaning

    An empty house shows everything. We clean inside cabinets, drawers, the oven, the fridge and closets, spot clean scuffed walls and leave it ready for a landlord's or buyer's walk-through. Send us your landlord's checklist and we'll follow it.

    • Inside cabinets, drawers and closets
    • Inside the oven and refrigerator
    • Baseboards, windowsills and blinds
    • Spot cleaning on walls and doors
    More on move-in and move-out cleaning

  • 04

    Vacation rental turnovers

    We clean between check-out and check-in, change linens, restock the supplies you keep on site and send photos when the unit is ready. We also flag damage or missing items so you can deal with them before the next guest arrives.

    • Scheduled from your booking calendar
    • Linen change and laundry
    • Restocking from your supplies
    • Photo report after each turnover
    More on vacation rental turnovers

  • 05

    Post-construction cleaning

    Construction dust gets onto every surface and keeps settling for days. We vacuum, wipe every surface from top to bottom, clean windows and tracks and remove stickers and residue from new fixtures. Bigger jobs may need a second pass after the dust settles.

    • Dust removed from walls, trim, vents and floors
    • Window, track and sill cleaning
    • Sticker and residue removal from fixtures
    • Cabinets and drawers wiped inside and out
    More on post-construction cleaning

  • 06

    Office cleaning

    We clean small offices, studios and storefronts after hours or before opening, on a schedule that fits your business. Restrooms, break rooms, floors and trash are covered every visit, from a checklist you can see.

    • Daily, weekly or every-other-week schedules
    • Restrooms and break rooms
    • Floors, trash and recycling
    • After-hours visits with key or code access
    More on office cleaning

  • 07

    Add-on services

    Add any of these to a regular visit or a one-time clean. They're priced per item, so you only pay for what you want done.

    • Inside the oven or refrigerator
    • Interior windows and tracks
    • Laundry and folding
    • Baseboards and blinds
    More on add-on services
