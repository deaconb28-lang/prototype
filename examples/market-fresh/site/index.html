Questions

Common questions

How much does house cleaning cost?

It depends on the size of the home, how often we come and its condition. Recurring cleaning for an average home usually runs $120 to $250 per visit. A first deep clean or a move-out clean costs more, often $250 to $500 or more. We give you a flat price before the first visit.

Do I need to be home?

No. Most customers give us a key, a door code or a lockbox code. Keys are stored securely and are never labeled with your address.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes, we bring all supplies and equipment, including vacuums and microfiber cloths. If you'd like us to use your products, or you have allergies or surfaces like marble that need special care, just tell us.

Will I get the same cleaner each time?

We schedule the same cleaner or team for your home whenever we can, so they learn how you like things done. If someone is out sick or on vacation, we'll let you know who's coming instead.

What isn't included in a regular cleaning?

Regular visits don't include the inside of the oven or fridge, interior windows, laundry or moving heavy furniture, though you can add most of those. We don't clean up biohazards, pest droppings or mold, and we don't work above step-stool height.
