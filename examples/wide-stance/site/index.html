Common questions

How much does garage door repair cost?

Spring replacement usually runs $150 to $350 for most residential doors, and cable, roller or off-track repairs often fall in a similar range. A new opener usually runs $350 to $900 installed. New doors start around $1,000 for a basic single door and go up with size, insulation and style. We give you a written price before we start.

Can I replace a garage door spring myself?

We don't recommend it. Torsion springs hold enough tension to lift a door that can weigh a few hundred pounds, and they cause serious injuries every year when they're released the wrong way. Leave the door closed and call a pro.

Should I replace both springs if only one broke?

Usually, yes. Springs on a two-spring door went in at the same time and have the same number of cycles on them, so the second one tends to break soon after. Replacing both at once saves you a second service call.

How long do garage door springs last?

Standard springs are rated for about 10,000 open-and-close cycles, which is roughly 7 to 10 years for a door used a few times a day. High-cycle springs last longer and cost a bit more up front.

My door starts to close, then goes back up. What's wrong?

Usually the safety sensors (the small photo eyes near the floor) are blocked, dirty or knocked out of line. Wipe the lenses, make sure both indicator lights are steady and nothing is in the way. If that doesn't fix it, the opener's force or travel settings may need adjusting.
