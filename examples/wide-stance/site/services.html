Services

Garage Doors services in Denver

Everything we do, with what's included. Call (303) 555-0108 if you don't see your job here.

  • 01

    Broken spring repair

    A broken spring is the most common reason a garage door won't open, and it's not a do-it-yourself job because the springs hold a lot of tension. We match the new springs to the door's weight and usually replace both, since the second one is close behind.

    • Torsion and extension springs
    • High-cycle springs that last longer
    • Door balanced and tested after install
    • Cables and bearings checked at the same visit
    More on broken spring repair

  • 02

    Opener repair

    Before replacing an opener, we check the usual causes: misaligned safety sensors, stripped gears, a worn trolley, a bad logic board or a door that's too heavy because of a weak spring. Many of these are a quick fix.

    • Safety sensor alignment and replacement
    • Gear, sprocket and trolley repair
    • Remote and keypad programming
    • All major opener brands
    More on opener repair

  • 03

    Cables, rollers and off-track doors

    A door that's crooked or off its track can fall, so leave it where it is and call us. We set the door back on the track, replace bent track sections, frayed cables and worn rollers, and get it running straight.

    • Off-track door reset
    • Cable and drum replacement
    • Nylon roller upgrades for quieter operation
    • Bent track and hinge replacement
    More on cables, rollers and off-track doors

  • 04

    New garage door openers

    Chain drives are the budget choice, belt drives are quieter, and wall-mount openers suit garages with high or crowded ceilings. We can add app control, battery backup and a keypad, and we set the force and travel limits so the safety reverse works the way it should.

    • Chain, belt and wall-mount openers
    • Wi-Fi and app control
    • Battery backup, required on new installs in some states
    • Keypads and remotes programmed
    More on new garage door openers

  • 05

    New garage doors

    We measure the opening, help you choose a style and insulation level and install the new door with new springs, tracks and weatherstripping. Ordered doors usually arrive in one to four weeks, installation takes half a day to a day, and we haul the old door away.

    • Insulated steel doors with R-values listed
    • Carriage-house and wood-look styles
    • Window and decorative hardware options
    • Old door removal and disposal included
    More on new garage doors

  • 06

    Tune-ups and safety inspections

    We lubricate hinges, rollers and springs, tighten hardware, test the door's balance and check that the safety reverse and photo eyes work. You get a short list of anything that's wearing out.

    • Lubrication of moving parts
    • Spring balance test
    • Safety reverse and photo eye test
    • Hardware tightened and weatherstripping checked
    More on tune-ups and safety inspections

  • 07

    Commercial doors

    We repair and install sectional and rolling steel doors and commercial operators for small businesses, shops and warehouses. Service calls are scheduled around your business hours.

    • Rolling steel and sectional doors
    • Commercial operators and controls
    • Annual fire door drop testing where required
    • Scheduled maintenance visits
    More on commercial doors
