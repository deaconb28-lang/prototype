Services

Electrical services in Fort Worth

Everything we do, with what's included. Call (817) 555-0188 if you don't see your job here.

  • 01

    Electrical repairs and troubleshooting

    Electrical problems rarely start where you notice them. We trace the circuit back to the cause, whether that's a loose connection, a failing breaker or an overloaded circuit, and fix it so it doesn't come back.

    • Circuit tracing and fault finding
    • Breaker and GFCI troubleshooting
    • Loose and burnt connection repair
    • Aluminum wiring repairs
    More on electrical repairs and troubleshooting

  • 02

    Outlets and switches

    Older homes often have too few outlets and no ground-fault protection where the code now requires it. We add outlets where you need them, replace two-prong outlets the right way and install GFCI and AFCI protection.

    • GFCI outlets for kitchens, baths, garages and outdoors
    • Two-prong to grounded outlet upgrades
    • Dimmers, smart switches and USB outlets
    • Dedicated circuits for appliances and home offices
    More on outlets and switches

  • 03

    Lighting

    We install new fixtures, add recessed lights to rooms that only had a lamp and wire outdoor and landscape lighting. We plan the layout with you so the light lands where you need it, not just in a grid.

    • Recessed and canless LED lights
    • Chandeliers and fixtures, including high ceilings
    • Under-cabinet and accent lighting
    • Outdoor, security and motion lighting
    More on lighting

  • 04

    Ceiling fans

    A ceiling fan needs a box rated to hold it, and a lot of old light fixture boxes aren't. We install the right box, run new wiring and switch legs if needed and balance the fan so it doesn't wobble.

    • Fan-rated boxes and braces
    • Separate fan and light control
    • New installs where there's no fixture today
    • Balancing and remote setup
    More on ceiling fans

  • 05

    Panel upgrades

    If you're adding an EV charger, heat pump or addition, your panel may not have the room or the capacity. We handle the permit and coordinate the shutoff with the utility, and most panel swaps are done in one day.

    • 100 to 200-amp service upgrades
    • Federal Pacific and Zinsco panel replacement
    • Permit, utility coordination and inspection
    • Whole-home surge protection added at the panel
    More on panel upgrades

  • 06

    EV charger installation

    A Level 2 charger usually adds 20 to 40 miles of range per hour, compared with a few miles from a standard outlet. We check your panel capacity first, run a dedicated circuit and mount the charger in the garage or outside.

    • Panel load check before we quote
    • Hardwired chargers or NEMA 14-50 outlets
    • Works with any charger brand you choose
    • Permit and inspection included
    More on ev charger installation

  • 07

    Generators

    A standby generator starts on its own within seconds of an outage and runs the circuits you choose, or the whole house. For a lower cost, we can install a transfer switch or interlock so a portable generator can safely run key circuits.

    • Whole-home standby generators
    • Automatic and manual transfer switches
    • Portable generator inlets and interlock kits
    • Gas line coordination, permit and annual service
    More on generators

  • 08

    Rewiring older homes

    Many homes built before the 1950s still have knob-and-tube wiring, and some insurers won't cover it. We rewire room by room, fishing new cable through walls where we can, and keep at least part of the house powered each night.

    • Knob-and-tube and cloth wiring replacement
    • Aluminum branch wiring remediation
    • Grounded circuits and code-required protection
    • Small access holes, patched or ready for paint
    More on rewiring older homes
