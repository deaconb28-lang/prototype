Questions

Common questions

How much does a new roof cost?

It depends mostly on the size and pitch of the roof, how many layers come off and the material. National cost data puts most asphalt shingle replacements between about $6,000 and $14,000, with large, steep or metal roofs running higher. We give you a written, itemized price after the inspection, and it doesn't change unless we find rotted decking.

Can you repair my roof, or does it need replacing?

Many leaks come from flashing or a cracked pipe boot, and a repair fixes them. Most repairs land between $400 and $1,800. If the shingles are curling, shedding granules or past about 20 years old, repairs start to become money spent twice, and we'll show you photos so you can judge for yourself.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most homes take one to three days once materials are delivered. Weather can move the start date, and we won't tear off a roof with rain in the forecast. You don't need to be home, but someone should be reachable by phone.

Will my homeowners insurance pay for it?

Insurance usually covers sudden damage from hail, wind or a fallen tree, not wear and age. We document the damage with photos and can meet your adjuster on the roof. You are responsible for your deductible, and in many states it's illegal for a roofer to waive it.
