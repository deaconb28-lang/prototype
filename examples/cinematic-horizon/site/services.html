Services

Roofing services in Boise

Everything we do, with what's included. Call (208) 555-0142 if you don't see your job here.

  • 01

    Roof repair

    Most leaks start at flashing, vents or a few lifted shingles, not the whole roof. We trace the water back to where it gets in, fix that spot and show you before and after photos. If a repair won't last, we'll tell you that too.

    • Leak tracing from the attic and the roof surface
    • Shingle, flashing, pipe boot and vent replacement
    • Closest available match to your existing shingles
    • Before and after photos with every repair
    More on roof repair

  • 02

    Roof replacement

    We strip the old roof down to the deck, replace any soft or rotted boards and install a complete system: underlayment, ice and water shield, flashing, vents and shingles. The price you sign is the price you pay unless we find rotted decking, and that is priced per sheet in your contract.

    • Architectural asphalt shingles, metal and synthetic options
    • Ice and water shield in valleys and along the eaves
    • Rotted decking replaced at a per-sheet price set up front
    • Magnetic nail sweep and full cleanup every day
    More on roof replacement

  • 03

    Storm damage and insurance claims

    After a storm we inspect, photograph every damaged area and tarp anything open to the weather. If it looks like a claim, we'll meet your adjuster on the roof and walk them through what we found. You pay your deductible; we don't waive it or hide it in the price.

    • Same-day tarping for open roofs
    • Photo report of hail and wind damage
    • We meet your adjuster on site
    • Repair or replacement once the claim is approved
    More on storm damage and insurance claims

  • 04

    Roof inspection

    We check shingles, flashing, vents, gutters and the attic for signs of leaks or poor ventilation. You get a photo report that says what's fine, what to keep an eye on and what needs fixing now. Buyers and sellers can get the same report during a home sale.

    • Shingle, flashing and sealant condition
    • Attic check for moisture, daylight and ventilation
    • Estimated remaining life of the roof
    • Written report you can share with an insurer or buyer
    More on roof inspection

  • 05

    Gutters and downspouts

    We form aluminum gutters on site from one continuous piece per run, so the only joints are at the corners. Downspouts are sized and placed to move water away from your foundation, and we can add guards if trees keep filling them.

    • 5 and 6 inch aluminum gutters in common colors
    • Downspout extensions to carry water away from the foundation
    • Gutter guards for homes under heavy trees
    • Repairs for sagging, leaking or pulled-away sections
    More on gutters and downspouts

  • 06

    Flat and low-slope roofs

    Shingles aren't made for roofs with little or no pitch, and that's where a lot of leaks start. We install and repair single-ply membranes and modified bitumen on porches, additions, garages and small commercial buildings.

    • TPO and EPDM single-ply membranes
    • Modified bitumen for small low-slope sections
    • Tapered insulation to move water off and stop ponding
    • Repairs and coatings to extend an older membrane
    More on flat and low-slope roofs

  • 07

    Skylights, chimneys and flashing

    Skylights and chimneys are the most common places for a roof to leak, and caulk alone won't hold for long. We replace step and counter flashing, install new flashing kits, or swap out old skylights while the roof is being done.

    • Step and counter flashing on chimneys
    • Skylight flashing kits and full skylight replacement
    • Crickets built behind wide chimneys to shed water
    • Pipe boots and vent flashing replaced
    More on skylights, chimneys and flashing
