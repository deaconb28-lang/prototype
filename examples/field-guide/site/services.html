Services

Landscaping services in Asheville

Everything we do, with what's included. Call (828) 555-0151 if you don't see your job here.

  • 01

    Lawn mowing and care

    We mow at the right height for your grass and the season, edge the walks and beds, trim what the mower can't reach and blow off the driveway before we leave. Fertilizer and weed control can be added on a set schedule.

    • Weekly or every-other-week visits
    • Edging along walks, drives and beds
    • Fertilizer and weed control programs
    • Aeration and overseeding in the fall
    More on lawn mowing and care

  • 02

    Spring and fall cleanups

    Spring cleanups get the beds and lawn ready for the season: debris out, perennials cut back, edges recut. Fall cleanups clear leaves before they smother the grass and get the yard ready for winter.

    • Leaf removal and haul-away
    • Perennial and ornamental grass cutback
    • Bed edging and weeding
    • Storm branch and debris cleanup
    More on spring and fall cleanups

  • 03

    Mulch and planting beds

    We pull weeds, recut the bed edges and spread mulch 2 to 3 inches deep, kept off tree trunks and plant stems so it doesn't cause rot. We can also replace tired or dead plants while we're there.

    • Hardwood, dyed and pine straw mulch
    • Bed edging and weed barrier where it helps
    • Seasonal color and annual plantings
    • Dead plant removal and replacement
    More on mulch and planting beds

  • 04

    Shrub and hedge trimming

    Cutting at the wrong time can cost you a year of flowers, so we trim spring bloomers after they flower and most hedges in late spring and again in summer. We stick to shrubs and small ornamental trees; larger tree work goes to a certified arborist.

    • Hedge shaping and size reduction
    • Ornamental tree pruning up to ladder height
    • Renewal pruning for overgrown shrubs
    • Clippings hauled away
    More on shrub and hedge trimming

  • 05

    Sprinklers and irrigation

    We install new systems zoned to how much water each area needs, and fix broken heads, leaking valves and controller problems on existing ones. Where it freezes, we start the system in spring and blow out the lines in fall so pipes don't crack.

    • New system design and installation
    • Head, valve and line repairs
    • Smart controllers that skip watering after rain
    • Spring startup and fall blowout
    More on sprinklers and irrigation

  • 06

    Sod and seeding

    A new lawn is only as good as the soil under it. We remove the old turf or weeds, grade for drainage, add topsoil where needed and lay sod or seed, then leave you a watering schedule you can follow.

    • Old lawn removal and grading
    • Topsoil and soil amendments
    • Sod or seed suited to your sun and shade
    • Written watering instructions for the first month
    More on sod and seeding

  • 07

    Landscape design and planting

    We start with what you want from the yard and how much upkeep you're willing to do, then draw a plan with plants that fit your soil, light and climate. Plants go in at the right depth with amended soil and a watering plan.

    • Site visit and scaled planting plan
    • Trees, shrubs and perennials from local nurseries
    • Low-water and native plant options
    • One-year plant guarantee when watered as directed
    More on landscape design and planting

  • 08

    Patios, walkways and retaining walls

    Most patios that sink or heave were built on too little base. We dig out, compact a gravel base in layers and set pavers or stone with edge restraints and polymeric sand. Retaining walls over about 4 feet are engineered, as most codes require.

    • Paver, natural stone and concrete patios
    • Walkways, steps and garden walls
    • Retaining walls with drainage behind them
    • Fire pits and seating walls
    More on patios, walkways and retaining walls
