Questions

Common questions

Don't see yours? Call (828) 555-0151 and ask.

How much does lawn mowing cost?

For a typical suburban lot, weekly mowing with edging and blowing usually runs $40 to $80 per visit. Bigger lots, steep slopes and lots of obstacles cost more. We give you a fixed per-visit price after seeing the yard.

How much does a paver patio cost?

Most paver patios run about $15 to $30 per square foot installed, so a 12 by 16 foot patio often lands between $3,000 and $6,000. Access, excavation, steps and the paver you choose move the price most. We itemize it in writing.

Do I need to be home?

No. For mowing and cleanups, we just need access through the gate and pets inside. For bigger projects, we'll meet you at the start and the end to walk through the work.

What happens if it rains on my mowing day?

We move the visit to the next dry day, usually within a day or two. Mowing wet grass tears the blades and leaves ruts, so it's worth the wait.
