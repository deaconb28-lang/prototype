Questions

Common questions

Don't see yours? Call (503) 555-0129 and ask.

How much does it cost to paint a room or a house?

An average bedroom, walls only, often runs $400 to $1,000, more with ceilings, trim and repairs. Exteriors vary widely; most single-family homes land between $4,000 and $10,000 depending on size, height, siding and how much prep the old paint needs. We give you a firm written price after we see it.

How long will it take?

Most single rooms take a day. A full interior usually takes three to five days, and most exteriors take three to seven working days, depending on prep and weather.

Do I need to move my furniture?

No. We move furniture to the middle of the room, cover it and the floors, and put it back when we're done. We do ask you to take down pictures and put away small items like lamps and anything breakable.

What paint do you use?

We use professional product lines from major brands like Sherwin-Williams and Benjamin Moore, and we name the exact product and sheen in your estimate. If you already have a brand or color you love, we're happy to use it.

How long will an exterior paint job last?

With good prep and quality paint, most exteriors last 5 to 10 years on wood siding and often longer on fiber cement or stucco. Sun-facing walls and horizontal surfaces wear first. Deck stains need recoating more often, usually every 2 to 4 years.
