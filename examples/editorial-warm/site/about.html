About Linden Street Painters

We put the prep, the paint and the price in writing, protect your floors and furniture, and walk the finished job with you before we call it done.

What we stand behind

Our guarantees

  • Workmanship warranty: 2 years interior, 3 years exterior

  • Firm price for the scope in your estimate

  • Touch-up walk-through before final payment

  • Daily cleanup, with furniture put back when we're done

Licenses and certifications

  • EPA Lead-Safe Certified Firm
  • PCA Accredited Painting Contractor
  • PCA Member
  • OSHA 10 Trained Crew Leads

How it works

What happens after you call

  1. 01

    Free estimate

    We walk through the rooms or around the house and talk about colors, finishes and timing.

  2. 02

    Written scope

    Your estimate lists every surface, the prep work and the paint product, with a firm price.

  3. 03

    Prep and paint

    We protect floors and furniture, do the prep and apply two finish coats.

  4. 04

    Walk-through

    We walk the job with you, mark anything that needs a touch-up with blue tape and fix it before we leave.
