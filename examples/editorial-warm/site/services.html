Services

Painting services in Portland

Everything we do, with what's included. Call (503) 555-0129 if you don't see your job here.

  • 01

    Interior painting

    A good paint job comes down to prep: filling nail holes, patching cracks, sanding, caulking trim and priming stains. We cover floors and furniture, use the right sheen for each room and clean up at the end of every day.

    • Two finish coats of a professional-grade paint
    • Patching, caulking and spot priming included
    • Ceilings, trim, doors and closets
    • Low-VOC paints available
    More on interior painting

  • 02

    Exterior painting

    Exterior paint fails when it goes on over loose paint, bare wood or damp siding. We wash, scrape, sand and prime bare spots, caulk gaps and replace small sections of rotted trim before painting. We schedule around the forecast and the temperature limits on the can.

    • Power washing and scraping
    • Rotted trim and siding repair
    • Primer on bare wood and repairs
    • Lead-safe work practices on homes built before 1978
    More on exterior painting

  • 03

    Cabinet painting

    We remove the doors and drawer fronts, degrease and sand, prime with a bonding primer and spray a cabinet-grade finish. The cabinet boxes are painted in place with the kitchen masked off. Most kitchens take about a week, for a fraction of the cost of new cabinets.

    • Doors and drawer fronts sprayed in a setup area or our shop
    • Degreasing and bonding primer
    • Cabinet-grade urethane or alkyd enamel
    • New hinges and pulls installed if you want them
    More on cabinet painting

  • 04

    Drywall repair

    We patch holes from doorknobs and old anchors, tape and mud cracks, and repair water-stained ceilings once the leak is fixed. We match the wall texture so the repair doesn't show.

    • Patches from small holes to full sheets
    • Ceiling water stain repair
    • Texture matching
    • Nail pops and tape seams fixed
    More on drywall repair

  • 05

    Deck and fence staining

    We wash the wood, strip or brighten it as needed and let it dry before staining. Clear, semi-transparent and solid stains each last a different amount of time, and we'll tell you what to expect from each.

    • Deck washing and stripping
    • Semi-transparent and solid stains
    • Fences, pergolas and railings
    • Loose boards and popped nails fixed first
    More on deck and fence staining

  • 06

    Wallpaper removal

    Wallpaper removal is slow work, and any glue left behind will show through new paint. We strip the paper, wash off the adhesive, seal any torn drywall facing and prime the walls before painting.

    • Removal by steaming or scoring and soaking
    • Adhesive washed off, not painted over
    • Torn drywall paper sealed and skim-coated
    • Primed and ready for paint
    More on wallpaper removal

  • 07

    Commercial painting

    We schedule around your business hours, work evenings or weekends when needed and turn rental units around in a few days between tenants. You get one written price for the whole scope, and we can provide a certificate of insurance before we start.

    • Offices, retail and restaurants
    • Apartment and rental turnovers
    • Evening and weekend scheduling
    • Low-odor paints for occupied spaces
    More on commercial painting
